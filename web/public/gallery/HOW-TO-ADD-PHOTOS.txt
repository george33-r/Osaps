OSAPS "Worn by real people" gallery
===================================

Only add a photo when the person in it has agreed in writing to appear on OSAPS,
and you own the photo or have a licence to use it. Celebrity or press photos may
not be used without a written agreement with both the person and the photographer.

1. Upload the image (JPG or WebP, about 1200 px tall) into this folder,
   e.g. amaka-asoebi.jpg
2. Add an entry to gallery.json:

{
  "items": [
    {
      "src": "amaka-asoebi.jpg",
      "alt": "A woman in a wine aso-oke iro and buba with a gold gele",
      "name": "Amaka, Abuja",
      "look": "Aso-ebi for her sister's wedding",
      "credit": "Photo by Tunde Studio, used with permission",
      "consent": true
    }
  ]
}

An entry without "consent": true or without a "credit" is never shown.
